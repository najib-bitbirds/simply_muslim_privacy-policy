Privacy Policy

Simply Muslim: Prayer & Dua

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Simply Muslim: Prayer & Dua ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. The App is provided by Najib Ahmed ("Developer"). We respect your privacy and have built the App so that your personal information stays on your device.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect, store, or share your personal data on any server.
  • Your location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device for prayer and dua reminders.
  • The App does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.
  • The App does not require you to create an account.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, advertising ID, or any other personal identifiers. We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

3. Permissions the App Uses

Location

The App may request access to your device's location (approximate or precise) so it can calculate accurate prayer times and show the Qibla direction for where you are. Location data is processed on your device only. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and we do not store it on any server. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings; in that case you can still use the App by choosing your city or setting your location manually, where available.

Notifications

The App may ask for permission to send you notifications, such as prayer time alerts and dua reminders. These notifications are created and scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to our servers. You can turn notifications off at any time in the App or in your device settings.

4. Data Storage

Any preferences or settings you choose in the App (for example calculation method, notification choices, or saved favorites) are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the App, this locally stored data is removed from your device.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services. The App is distributed through Google Play, which has its own privacy practices that apply to your use of the Play Store.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data Security

Because your data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, the main protection for it is your device's own security. We encourage you to keep your device and operating system up to date.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. You are in full control of the permissions you grant and can change or remove them at any time through your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
